Weekly Meal Prep Agreement

Welcome! Below is a simple outline of our weekly workflow, fees, and expectations so everything runs seamlessly.

1. Rates & Payment

  • Hourly Rate: $40 per hour for all on-site prep, cooking, packaging, and kitchen cleanup (typically 3 to 5 hours per cook day).
  • Travel & Mileage: Mileage is billed at standard IRS rates for travel to and from your home.
  • Payment Methods: I accept Zelle, Venmo, Cash, or Check.
  • Payment Terms: Upon completion, I will provide you with the amount due. Payment is due upon receipt.

2. Weekly Schedule & Process

  • Weekend Menu Prep: I will send your proposed menu every Saturday or Sunday morning.
  • Menu Deadline: Please review and send your menu approvals or adjustments by Sunday night at 11:59 PM.
  • Grocery List: I will provide you with a full, detailed grocery list at least 48 hours prior to your scheduled cook day.
  • Client Food Purchase: You are responsible for purchasing all grocery items on the list prior to my arrival.

3. Cook Day Expectations

  • Scheduled Service: Cook day is scheduled weekly starting on your agreed date.
  • Time in Kitchen: I will be in your kitchen for approximately 3 to 5 hours on cook day.
  • Kitchen Access: Please ensure kitchen counters are clear, the sink is empty, and adequate fridge space is available for your prepped meals.
  • Spotless Cleanup: I respect your home! I will wash all pots, pans, and tools used during prep, wipe down all kitchen surfaces, and leave your kitchen completely spotless.

4. Photos & Video Content

  • I love sharing behind-the-scenes glimpses of meal prep on social media to grow my business.
  • Your Privacy: I will never reveal your name, address, family members, or house layout. Permission options are reviewed directly with each client during onboarding.

5. Cancellations & Rescheduling

  • Notice Required: Because cook day slots are reserved weekly, please provide at least 48 hours notice if you need to skip or reschedule a week.
  • Late Cancellations: Cancellations made with less than 48 hours notice will be charged 50% of the estimated service fee for that day.

6. Dietary & Health Safety

  • Please notify me of all food allergies, intolerances, and dietary preferences prior to menu finalization.
